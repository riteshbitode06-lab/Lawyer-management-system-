⚖️ Lawyer Management System

📌 Project Description

The Lawyer Management System is a web-based application designed to simplify and organize the management of lawyers, clients, cases, court hearings, appointments, documents, and payments.

The system provides a centralized platform where lawyers can manage their legal cases and client information efficiently while reducing paperwork and manual record keeping.

🎯 Objectives

* Manage lawyer and client information.
* Maintain complete case records.
* Track case status and progress.
* Manage court hearing dates.
* Schedule client appointments.
* Upload and manage legal documents.
* Track lawyer fees and client payments.
* Provide secure access based on user roles.
* Generate useful reports.

🚀 Features

👨‍⚖️ Lawyer Management

* Add and update lawyer details.
* View lawyer profiles.
* Manage assigned cases.
* Track case progress.

👤 Client Management

* Register new clients.
* Maintain client contact details.
* View client case history.
* Manage client-related documents.

📁 Case Management

* Create new cases.
* Store case number, type, court, and description.
* Assign cases to lawyers.
* Update case status.
* Track important case dates.

📅 Hearing & Appointment Management

* Schedule court hearings.
* Schedule lawyer-client meetings.
* View upcoming hearings and appointments.
* Maintain hearing history.

📄 Document Management

* Upload case-related documents.
* Organize documents according to cases.
* View and download documents.

💰 Payment Management

* Record lawyer fees.
* Track received and pending payments.
* Maintain payment history.
* Generate billing information.

🔐 Authentication & Security

* Secure login system.
* Role-based access.
* Admin, Lawyer, and Client access.
* Protection of confidential case information.

👥 User Roles

Role	Responsibilities
Admin	Manage users, lawyers, clients, cases, and system settings
Lawyer	Manage clients, cases, hearings, appointments, and documents
Client	View case details, hearings, appointments, and payment information
